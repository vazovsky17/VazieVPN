Vazie VPN is a VPN client for the VLESS protocol. It builds an encrypted tunnel between your phone and a server, so the network you are on sees a connection to that server and nothing about the sites you open.

YOUR OWN CONFIGURATIONS
• Add a VLESS link: paste it from the clipboard, scan a QR code or open the link in the app.
• REALITY and TLS, over TCP, WebSocket, gRPC and XHTTP.
• Configurations stay on the device, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. They are never uploaded — not to us, not to anyone.
• Each server's latency is shown right in the list.

VAZIE SERVERS — VPN PLUS
No server of your own? Connect to Vazie's servers with VPN Plus: one month or one year, paid once, nothing renews by itself. Sign in with a code sent by email — no password. New servers open as subscribers grow.

ALWAYS AT HAND
• Two home-screen widgets, in light and dark.
• A Quick Settings tile.
• Your recent connections as app shortcuts.
• A notification with the tunnel's state and a disconnect button.

STRAIGHT ABOUT DATA
• No ads, no behaviour analytics, no advertising identifiers, no location.
• No log of the sites you visit or of what you connected to.
• An account is optional: your own configurations never need one.
• Crash reports are off until you turn them on.

DESIGN
Two palettes — Milk and Night Indigo, a choice of app icons, English and Russian, TalkBack and large-font support.
